## Purpose
Contractor lifecycle: onboarding stages, document upload/verification, team assignment, project linkage, invoice/progress-claim/payment tracking, RAG compliance.

## Critical Rules
- ALWAYS use `LOWER()` for email lookups — contractor emails are not case-normalised at ingestion
- NEVER delete a contractor with linked projects — `contractors-delete.ts` blocks on a `contractor_projects` count; do not weaken
- Routes use `withAuth()` only. There is no `withPermission()` guard today — add one if you introduce role-scoped operations
