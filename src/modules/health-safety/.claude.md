## Gate Check Logic
Hard blockers (cannot assign): missing/expired `safety_policy`, `liability_insurance`, `safety_plan`; major/fatal incident in last 12 months; score <50%; training <70%.
```typescript
const gate = await checkContractorGate(contractorId);
if (!gate.can_assign) throw new Error(gate.blockers.join(', '));
```

## Scoring Weights
Documents 25% · Incidents 30% · Training 15% · Corrective Actions 15% · Audits 15%
RAG: Red <50% · Amber 50-79% · Green ≥80%

## Critical Rules
- H&S incidents are maintenance tickets (type `hse_incident`/`hse_near_miss`) with `hs_ticket_details` extension — not a separate ticket system
- DoL reporting required for major/fatal incidents within 24 hours
- `gateService.ts` still uses `@/lib/db-neon` shim — migrate to `pg.Pool` when touching it
