## Purpose
Vehicle fleet management with driver check-in/check-out, VLM-powered photo analysis, GPS tracking, and maintenance scheduling.

## Critical Rules
- NEVER skip VLM validation for check-in photos
- ALWAYS use offline sync for field operations
- Use `sharp` to resize images before VLM (max 1024x768)
- **First-time check-in REQUIRES calibration** (odometer, fuel, dashboard photo)
