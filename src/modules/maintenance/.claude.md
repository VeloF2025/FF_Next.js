## Purpose
Maintenance ticket system with QContact integration, team assignment, escalation workflows, and verification tracking.

## Critical Rules
- NEVER bypass escalation rules for overdue tickets
- ALWAYS sync with QContact for external tickets
- Check QA readiness before closing tickets
