## Purpose
Meeting management integrating Fireflies.ai transcripts with auto-sync, participant matching, and Communications Portal display.

## Critical Rules
- Fireflies `meeting_attendees` is usually EMPTY — use `speakers` field as primary participant source
- Non-admin users matched by BOTH email AND name (speakers have names only, no emails)
- Two data pipelines: `MeetingsDashboard.tsx` (/meetings) and `useCommunications.ts` (/communications?tab=meetings) — keep in sync
- UPSERT must update ALL fields on conflict, not just title/summary
